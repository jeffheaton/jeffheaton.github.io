Contact Jeff Heaton

Thanks for reaching out. I genuinely enjoy hearing from readers, students, researchers, and fellow AI enthusiasts.

Members of the media: interview and comment requests are very welcome. Email me with PRESS in the subject line and I will respond the same day.

For the quickest response, message me on LinkedIn. I receive a large number of connection requests, so including a quick note about why you are reaching out helps a great deal. It lets me spot genuine messages right away and keeps your request from getting lost in the pile.

You can also email me directly at jeff@jeffheaton.com. Fair warning: that inbox attracts a mountain of spam and solicitations, so an email reply may take me longer. If your message is time-sensitive, LinkedIn is the better path.

Sales pitches and other solicitations: please send these through LinkedIn rather than email.

A small apology in advance: given the volume of mail and solicitations I receive, I am not always able to respond personally. Please know that genuine messages really are read and appreciated, even when I cannot reply to every one.

Either way, a clear subject line and a sentence or two about what you need go a long way. I read everything that is not trying to sell me something!
